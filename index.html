Self-test audiometry

⚠ Not a medical test. This tool is not a replacement for clinical audiometry. It is uncalibrated, so the numbers are not true hearing levels (dB HL) and cannot diagnose or rule out hearing loss. If you are worried about your hearing, see an audiologist or doctor. Raise the level in small steps and stop if any sound is uncomfortably loud.

Before you start

  1. Use wired, closed (over-ear) headphones plugged into the 3.5 mm jack. Avoid Bluetooth.
  2. Set the system volume to maximum and turn off any sound enhancements or equalizer.
  3. Turn off any audio accessibility settings about treating stereo sound as mono.
  4. Sit in a quiet room. Check which side of the headphones is left and which is right.
  5. Start with the level too quiet to hear, then press Play pulse and raise it 2.5 dB at a time.
  6. Your threshold is the quietest level where you can hear the tone.
